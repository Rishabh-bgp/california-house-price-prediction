About

  • Model: scikit-learn gradient boosting (Huber loss, 400 trees, depth 5) on log value; 5% / 95% quantile models (150 trees, depth 4) give the range.
  • Repeated 5-fold CV: see the results table, including a harsher spatial CV.
  • Data: California Housing, R. K. Pace & R. Barry (1997), Sparse Spatial Autoregressions, Statistics & Probability Letters 33, 291–297; via StatLib and scikit-learn.
  • Developed as part of my training with KF Property Network Pte Ltd (KFPN), a Knight Frank Singapore subsidiary, and submitted as my capstone for the NIELIT Ropar × IIT Ropar AI & ML programme.
